About Md. A. Latif

A Level Mathematics teacher (CAIE 9709, Pure Mathematics 3) — Senior Faculty at Glenrich International School, B.Sc (Hons), M.Sc (Mathematics). This page states only what his public channels publish.

W
Profile

Teaching Cambridge mathematics, in school and beyond

Md. Abdul Latif (published as "Md. A. Latif" on YouTube) teaches the Cambridge mathematics pathway — IGCSE and CAIE A Level — with a focused Pure Mathematics 3 lecture library for the 9709 syllabus. Between January and May 2021 he recorded thirteen complete P3 lectures: an opening walkthrough of the syllabus, then full treatments of algebra, logarithms and exponentials, trigonometry, and differentiation.

Senior Faculty at Glenrich International School — and former teacher at The Aga Khan School. More than 20 years of teaching experience.

As published on his own Facebook page's flyers (Study Hub flyer, 2025; Cambridge Mathematics poster, June 2025).

The coaching offer, in his own words

His page advertises a Study Hub programme for Cambridge-path students. The flyer lists: topic-wise assessments and personalized feedback, booklets for structured and progressive learning, intensive past-paper practice, and real-world application with conceptual clarity. His teaching approach, as printed on the flyer: concept-based learning, step-by-step problem solving, regular practice with exam technique, and regular assessment & feedback.

The current programme covers A Level Pure Mathematics 1, 2 and 3 (9709) plus Mechanics and Statistics, and IGCSE Extended Mathematics (0580) and International Additional Mathematics (0606). At Study Hub, Uttara, Grade 9–10 Cambridge General & Additional Mathematics classes run Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday — the session schedule is published as photos on the page.

His public teaching presence runs on two channels: the lecture library on YouTube, and this Facebook page serving Dhaka students.

B. Sc(Hon) M. Sc (Mathematics)

The qualification line exactly as he published it in his official syllabus video title — "CAIE A Level Mathematics 9709 Syllabus 2021 by Md. A. Latif B. Sc(Hon) M. Sc (Mathematics)" — YouTube, 24 January 2021.

Beyond the lines quoted above, his public channels publish no further degrees or records — so none are stated anywhere on this website.
