About the teacher

Mathematics, taught step by step

Md. A. Latif teaches CAIE A Level Mathematics (9709), focused on Pure Mathematics 3. His recorded lectures walk through each P3 topic — algebra, logarithms and exponentials, trigonometry and differentiation — in a calm, exam-oriented sequence, starting from the official syllabus overview.
